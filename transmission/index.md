## What this lens makes visible

The transmission model pictures communication as a message moving from a sender, through a channel, to a receiver. Noise can interrupt the journey. This is useful when diagnosing a broken upload, a garbled text, or an AI system that drops an important qualification from a summary.

For your analysis, introduce the phenomenon you are studying and identify its sender, message, channel, receiver, and forms of noise. Ask what the model clarifies when the problem looks like a failure of delivery.

## Where the model stops

The mathematical model is intentionally not a theory of human understanding. It does not tell us why a phrase matters to one person and not another, or why a perfectly delivered message can still feel dismissive. A humanist critique can bring bodies, histories, relationships, emotion, and responsibility back into view.

> A message can arrive intact and still fail as communication.

Use this section to explain what the model misses in your example. What counts as noise if the problem is not distortion, but unequal attention or unequal power?
